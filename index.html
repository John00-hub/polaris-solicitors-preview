The Firm

Thirteen years of counsel
built on judgement.

Polaris Solicitors was founded in 2013 as a multidimensional business law practice — partners, associates and dedicated support staff, working from a modern office in the Wuse 2 commercial district of Abuja.

Our clients are founders and multinationals, developers and financial institutions, public bodies and private individuals. What they have in common is that the matter matters: a transaction that has to close cleanly, a regulator that has to be satisfied, a dispute that has to be resolved without derailing the business behind it.

We are built to be that fixed point — clear advice, disciplined process, and an honest view of the odds before you commit. From Lagos to Abuja and across all thirty-six states, we bring the same standard of diligence to every brief.

Practice Areas

Eight disciplines.
One standard of attention.

A full-service offering for clients who need a single counsel of record across the commercial life of a business.

01

Corporate & Commercial

Formation, governance and structuring for businesses that intend to grow without compliance debt.

  • CAC incorporation & restructuring
  • Shareholder & investment agreements
  • Commercial contracts, NDAs, JVs
02

Dispute Resolution

Strategic advocacy in court, and where it serves the client better, an early settlement.

  • Commercial & civil litigation
  • Arbitration & mediation
  • Debt recovery & enforcement
03

Intellectual Property

Protecting the names, marks and inventions that make a business worth more than its balance sheet.

  • Trademark & patent filings
  • IP licensing & assignment
  • Enforcement & opposition
04

Property & Real Estate

Conveyancing and development counsel that treats title risk as the first question, not the last.

  • Title investigation & perfection
  • Leases & development agreements
  • Estate & facility structuring
05

Regulatory & Government

Navigating policy, licensing and the agencies that sit between a business and its market.

  • Licensing & permits
  • Regulatory compliance reviews
  • Policy & stakeholder engagement
06

Banking & Finance

Transaction counsel shaped by first-hand knowledge of how lenders and regulators actually decide.

  • Secured lending & security perfection
  • Corporate finance & advisory
  • Recovery & restructuring
07

Capital Markets

Support across securities, offerings and the regulatory steps that stand between a deal and a listing.

  • Offer documentation
  • SEC & exchange engagement
  • Investor & disclosure compliance
08

Trade & Investment

Cross-border support for investors and corporates entering, expanding within, or exiting the Nigerian market.

  • Market entry structures
  • Cross-border agreements
  • Labour & industrial relations

How We Work

From first call to resolution.

A defined path, so you always know which stage your matter is at and what happens next.

01

Consultation

A focused first conversation — in chambers, by phone or by video. You leave with a clear view of your position, not a sales pitch.

02

Assessment

We review documents, identify the risks worth your attention, and set out the realistic routes available to you.

03

Strategy & Engagement

Scope, fee basis and timeline agreed in writing. A named lead counsel takes ownership of the file from that point.

04

Resolution

Execution through to closing, judgment or settlement — with the file documented so you are never left holding loose ends.
